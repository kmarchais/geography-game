# Atlas country list

Verified on 26 October 2026 against the [UN member-state list](https://www.un.org/about-us/member-states) and [UN non-member observer states](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/non-member-states).

The quiz contains exactly the 193 UN member states plus the two non-member observer states. Atlas represents the Holy See with Vatican City and labels the State of Palestine as Palestine. Common country names can differ from UN names, for example Cape Verde / Cabo Verde and Nauru / Naoero.

This selection defines the quiz. UN membership and observer status are different from diplomatic recognition. The [UN explains that recognition is granted by states, not by the UN itself](https://www.un.org/about-us/about-un-membership).

Territories and other states, including Kosovo and Taiwan, are outside this quiz list. Boundaries are generalized Natural Earth data and are not an official UN boundary endorsement.
